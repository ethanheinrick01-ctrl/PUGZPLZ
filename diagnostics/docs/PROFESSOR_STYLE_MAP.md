## What the lab adds that her historical tests did not show

- **Case-based integration (Boss drills).** COMD 4756 is a clinical-process course: interview a parent, run an OME, read a score report. The two Boss drills cover 6 cases (Maya from her Group B parent handout; your Ethan Miller interview; a score report; a bilingual referral; an acute-care adult; choosing a test with the ASHA decision process). This is the lab's choice for this course, labeled integration, not a prediction.
- **Visual identification.** Click-the-region items on her bell curve, the oral cavity and molar classes. Her slides and the OPE video are visual. This is not a claim about her exam format.
- **Procedures.** CA with borrowing (convention stated), scale conversion, words per minute, TRMR, adjusted age.
