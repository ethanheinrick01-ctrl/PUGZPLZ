## Corrections and boundaries

- Lake Talker is corrected to late talker. SmartArt text was recovered in addition to ordinary slide text.
- MLU is not an exact developmental age or standalone diagnosis. Conflicting textbook language about irregular-plural counting is not keyed.
- Typical aphasia/RHD profiles are not universal individual rules. Associated apraxia is not assumed in every Broca's profile.
- The October21 concussion transcript contains contradictory fragments. [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/heads-up/about/) identifies concussion as a TBI.
- Consciousness states are not brain death; the [AAN/ACRM guideline](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6139814/) supports careful multidisciplinary, standardized serial assessment. This correction is disclosed separately from course scope.
- Medical-condition entries preserve the supplied associations and possible qualifiers; they are prompts for assessment, not patient diagnoses or exhaustive disease descriptions.
- Narration is original synthetic educational speech using Ethan's local voice workflow, not the instructor's recording. Acoustic/ASR checks and subjective listening are distinct.

- Swallowing phases identify functions; observed signs alone do not establish physiological safety. Course VFSS preference is preserved as course wording, not a universal ranking above FEES. Absence of cough does not exclude silent aspiration, and developmental milestones do not clear an individual for feeding.
- Fluency thresholds are course diagnostic considerations. Integrate representative samples, differential findings, secondary behaviors and impact rather than diagnose from a cutoff alone. Total index, type index and share of disfluencies use different denominators.
- PFD concerns age-inappropriate intake with dysfunction in one or more medical, nutritional, feeding-skill or psychosocial domains. PFD and dysphagia are separate diagnoses that may co-occur; multidisciplinary findings must be integrated.
