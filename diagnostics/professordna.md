Ask one concrete decision. Brief cases supply the discriminating observation: stable repetitions and intact prosody support a functional profile; inconsistency alone does not establish CAS. Use plausible same-category choices, parallel phrasing and comparable detail. Do not make the correct choice systematically longer. Shuffle choices and save their order in the activity; keep first answers immutable.

Use negative stems to distinguish neighboring features or limit a conclusion to its evidence. Example: the NOT-justified conclusion from a concerning cluster is an already established diagnosis. Use T/F swaps in a single critical term or qualifier: functional versus organic; sufficient versus insufficient. Correct false statements explicitly after checking.

Matching connects observed behavior to the feature, with independent saved row and term order. Listings specify the requested number or components; pay per component using an explicit rubric. Self-scoring is transparent and separate from automated mastery. Every MC choice gets a rationale; every question gets an exact source, supporting passage and tested-decision label.

The NU545 layout and chapter-first film flow carry over; nursing content, course identity, counts and weights do not. Use unchanged Diagnostics slide figures and honest guided comparison. No invented acoustic displays. Learning, practice, review, cases and the mock share the established Diagnostics progress record. Do not erase old competencies or rewrite answered snapshots.
